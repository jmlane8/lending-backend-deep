KEY DISCUSSION POINTS
─────────────────────

1. PLATFORM ACCESS
   - Only legally contracted partners are permitted to submit loan applications through the API.
   - No submissions are accepted from non-contracted parties.


2. CUSTOMER DATA – MANDATORY FIELDS
   - Government-issued ID photo (must not be expired)
   - National ID number
   - Full legal name
   - Selfie photo
   - Phone number
   - Gender
   - Date of birth (customer must be 18 years of age or older)
   - Place of birth
   - Marital status — accepted values: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed
   - Occupation — accepted values: Employee, Entrepreneur, Civil Servant
   - Work duration (in years)
   - Monthly income
   - Number of dependents
   - Domicile address: street, district, city, province

   Optional:
   - Email address


3. SPOUSE DATA (Required if marital status is Married)
   Mandatory:
   - National ID number
   - Full legal name
   - Date of birth (must be 18 years of age or older)
   - Phone number

   Optional:
   - Email address
   - Government-issued ID photo


4. CAR COLLATERAL DATA – MANDATORY FIELDS
   - Vehicle brand
   - Vehicle model
   - License plate number
   - Ownership holder (customer or spouse)
   - Manufacturing year
   - Note: All collateral fields must match master data. Submissions with values outside
     master data are rejected.


5. LOAN DATA – MANDATORY FIELDS
   - Loan amount — minimum: 500, maximum: 200,000
   - Loan tenure
   - Both loan amount and tenure must be selected from master data.


6. MASTER DATA
   - Maintained by the business team.
   - Defines valid: vehicle brands, models, manufacturing years, loan tenures, and
     geographic coverage areas.
   - Submissions originating from locations outside defined coverage areas will be rejected.
   - A license plate is considered valid for submission only if it is not currently tied
     to an active loan.


7. RESUBMISSION RULES
   - After a rejection: the partner must wait 30 days before resubmitting the same application.
   - After an approval: the customer's existing loan must be fully settled before a new
     application for the same collateral can be submitted.


8. PARTNER RULES AND COMMISSION
   - Partners may submit an unlimited number of loan applications.
   - A high rejection ratio is flagged as a concern and may be reviewed.
   - Commission is paid per approved loan, based on the individual partner agreement.
   - Commission amounts are confidential and will not be disclosed through the API or
     any business documentation.


9. LOAN STATUS TRACKING
   - Partners can track submitted loans via a monthly report or through a separate status API.
   - The status API is considered out of scope for the current project.


10. POST-SUBMISSION ADJUSTMENTS
    - Loan amount and tenure may be adjusted after submission following a survey and
      risk assessment.
    - These adjustments are not part of the submission process and are not a concern
      for API design at this stage.
