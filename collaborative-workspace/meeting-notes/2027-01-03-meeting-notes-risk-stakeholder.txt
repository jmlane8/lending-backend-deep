harry is risk officer - handles risk scoring side. not involved in biz rules, not tech.

RISK SCORING
- every loan app goes thru risk scoring process - managed by risk team
- scoring model classifies apps into 4 categories:
  VERY_LOW - best risk profile, strongest recommendation for approval
  LOW
  HIGH
  VERY_HIGH - highest risk, strongest recommendation for rejection

- important: scores are RECOMMENDATIONS only - not automatic approve/reject
- final decision = regional risk committee, usually 2-3 members
- committee review is a SEPARATE process - not part of submission or scoring

asked if he can disclose scoring methodology - NO. proprietary. only risk team knows the calculation.
can be updated anytime without notice.
also wont say which specific data points affect the score - just tells us what data they need.

DATA REQUIRED FOR RISK SCORING
(mandatory unless noted otherwise)
- customer gov ID number
- spouse gov ID number (if applicable / married)
- monthly household income - customer
- monthly household income - spouse (if applicable)
- number of dependents
- customer phone number
- spouse phone number - OPTIONAL
- vehicle brand
- vehicle model
- vehicle year of manufacture
- vehicle ownership holder (customer, spouse, or another party)
  -> "another party" - so not always customer or spouse, worth noting
- ownership/legal documents - existence AND validity, including whether any are expired
- passport number - OPTIONAL

asked about special cases or exceptions - he confirmed there might be some but formulas are undisclosed.
no further details given (not allowed to disclose, cannot force).
